About Melila Grama Panchayat

Introduction and geographical identity

Introduction

Melila Grama Panchayat is a rural local self-government institution situated in Kollam district of Kerala, India. It comes under Vettikkavala Block Panchayat and Kottarakkara Taluk.


The region represents the characteristic rural landscape of Kerala, with settlements, agricultural activities, roads, vegetation and other geographical features. Its natural and human-made resources contribute to the socio-economic development of the area.


This interactive geographical information platform provides an opportunity to explore the spatial characteristics of Melila Grama Panchayat. It integrates geographical information with interactive mapping technology.

Geographical Setting

Melila is situated towards the eastern boundary of Kottarakkara Taluk in Kollam district. It shares geographical boundaries with Vilakkudy and Thalavoor villages in Pathanapuram Taluk and Vettikkavala, Chakkuvarackal, Mylom and Kottarakkara villages in Kottarakkara Taluk.


The National Highway 744 and the Kottayam-Thiruvananthapuram State Highway pass through the region. The landscape consists of rural settlements, agricultural land, vegetation and transport networks.
